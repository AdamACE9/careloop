CareLoop

The companion that actually checks in.
An agentic AI that phones older people about their medications, listens to how they answer, and decides on its own when their family needs to know.
Built by Adam Ahmedcareloop--careloop-adam.europe-west4.hosted.app
01 · The problem

Reminders can't hear the one word that matters.

What a reminder app records
“Yes, taken.”
A tap on a notification. No way to tell a confident yes from a guess, or a pill taken from a pill remembered.
What she actually said
“I think I took it.”
On a blood thinner, that uncertainty is the signal. Twice in a week, it's a pattern someone should know about.
Reminders only pingThey tell someone to do a thing. They never find out whether it happened.
Families find out lateA missed pattern usually surfaces at the pharmacy, or the hospital.
Monitoring feels like surveillanceNearly half of older adults are uncomfortable being monitored, even when the safety benefit is clear.

Not a reminder. A phone call that thinks.

03 · How a call works

It rings like a real call.

1
The server rings the phone
A scheduler sends a high-priority push. Android shows a full-screen incoming call, on the lock screen.
2
A live voice conversation
Gemini Live, streamed directly from the phone with a single-use token that expires in 15 minutes. The API key never ships in the app.
3
Cara acts through tools, mid-sentence
Check an interaction, record a dose, log a reading, note something for next time, report an emergency.
4
The call is written, then reasoned about
The check-in is saved and the reasoning engine decides what happens next.

12 · Safety and honesty

Hard lines it never crosses.

Never diagnoses, never changes a dose
It surfaces known interactions and routes to a pharmacist or GP.
Emergencies stop the check-in
Chest pain or a fall: tell her to call emergency services, and alert family at once.
Never invents what it doesn't know
With no medications on record, it asks what she takes instead of guessing.
Honest about being an AI
If asked, Cara says so plainly. Warm, never "sweetie".
Private by construction
No names, medications or transcripts ever reach the logs. The API key never leaves the server.
Example data is always labelled
A health record that could be fake must say so. Real data is the default.
